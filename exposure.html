Third-Party Exposure

Which third-party services a web page loads or talks to, each rated by what that vendor could do to the page's visitors if it were compromised. Each scan loads the page in Chromium, records every request and who made it, and rates the result. It maps attack surface; it does not detect whether any vendor is compromised.

Scans are run by tpscan from this repository, before any consent choice and again after accepting and after rejecting cookies.
